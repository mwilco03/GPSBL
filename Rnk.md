# Air National Guard Promotion Requirements Matrix

*ANG (Title 32) | Per ANGI 36-2502 (Enlisted) and ANGI 36-2504 (Officer)*

---

## Enlisted Promotions (E-2 through E-9)

ANG enlisted promotions are **position vacancy (PVP) based**, not competitive testing. A member must occupy a valid Unit Manpower Document (UMD) position at the higher grade and be recommended by their commander. The Adjutant General (TAG) is the promotion authority for MSgt and above.

| Grade | Rank | Min TIG | Min TIS | Skill Level | PME Required | Promotion Method | Notes |
|-------|------|---------|---------|-------------|--------------|------------------|-------|
| E-2 | Amn | 6 mo | None | N/A | None | Commander recommendation | Must meet AF standards |
| E-3 | A1C | 10 mo | None | N/A | None | Commander recommendation | 6-yr enlistees: promote after tech school |
| E-4 | SrA | 12 mo as A1C | 24 mo | 3-level | None | Commander recommendation | TIS computed from IADT entry date |
| E-5 | SSgt | 6 mo | 3 yr | 5-level | ALS (before sew-on) | PVP; commander recommendation | Must occupy valid UMD vacancy |
| E-6 | TSgt | 24 mo | 5 yr | 7-level | NCOA | PVP; commander recommendation | Must be sole occupant of UMD position |
| E-7 | MSgt | 24 mo | 8 yr | 7-level | NCOA | PVP; TAG approval | Must be sole occupant; CCAF degree req'd |
| E-8 | SMSgt | 20 mo | 11 yr | 7- or 9-level | SNCOA | PVP; TAG approval | CCAF degree req'd; ~2% force cap |
| E-9 | CMSgt | 21 mo | 14 yr | 9-level | SNCOA | PVP; TAG approval | CCAF degree req'd; ~1.25% force cap |

**ANG-specific notes:**
- TIS is computed from the date the member enters Initial Active Duty for Training (IADT)
- SrA TIG/TIS differs from active duty: 12 mo TIG as A1C and 24 mo TIS (per ANGI 36-2502)
- TSgt and above must be the sole occupant of a valid UMD position vacancy
- No member may be reassigned as excess/overgrade to create a vacancy for another member's promotion
- State manning percentages apply (e.g., 125% max for TSgt, 120% max for MSgt)

## Officer Promotions (O-2 through O-7)

ANG officer promotions are a **function of the State**, with Federal Recognition granted by the Chief of the National Guard Bureau acting for the Secretary of the Air Force. Promotions to Capt through Lt Col may occur via position vacancy (PVP) or Reserve of the Air Force Promotion Board (ROPB).

| Grade | Rank | Min TIG | Typical TIS at Promotion | Promotion Method | PME / Key Requirements |
|-------|------|---------|--------------------------|------------------|------------------------|
| O-2 | 1st Lt | 2 yr DOR | 2 yr | Virtually automatic | Meet AF standards; Federal Recognition |
| O-3 | Capt | 2 yr DOR (1st Lt) | 4 yr | PVP or ROPB | Meet AF standards; Federal Recognition |
| O-4 | Maj | 3 yr (as O-3) | ~10 yr | PVP or ROPB; Federal Recognition Board | SOS; must occupy UMD vacancy |
| O-5 | Lt Col | 3 yr (as O-4) | ~16 yr | PVP or ROPB; Federal Recognition Board | ACSC / equiv.; must occupy UMD vacancy |
| O-6 | Col | 3 yr (as O-5) | ~22 yr | Certificate of Eligibility; Senate confirmation | AWC / equiv.; Federal Recognition required |
| O-7 | Brig Gen | 1 yr (as O-6) | Varies | Statutory; Senate confirmed | Joint duty; senior PME; NGB/state selection |

**ANG-specific notes:**
- Promotion is tied to occupying a UMD position authorized at the higher grade
- Officers are not entitled to promotion simply because they occupy a higher-graded billet or due to seniority
- Federal Recognition must be extended before an officer is promoted as a Reserve of the Air Force
- The ROPB (Reserve of the Air Force Promotion Board) considers officers not promoted via PVP
- No Below-the-Zone promotions in the ANG (unlike active duty)
- Colonel requires a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) process and Senate confirmation

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| TIS | Time in Service: computed from IADT entry date for ANG members |
| TIG | Time in Grade: time served at current rank from Date of Rank (DOR) |
| UMD | Unit Manpower Document: authorizes positions and grades within a unit |
| PVP | Position Vacancy Promotion: promotion into a higher-graded UMD slot |
| ROPB | Reserve of the Air Force Promotion Board: considers ANG/Reserve officers for promotion |
| TAG | The Adjutant General: state-level promotion authority for MSgt and above |
| IADT | Initial Active Duty for Training: BMT + tech school; establishes TIS start date |
| ALS | Airman Leadership School: ~24-day NCO preparatory course |
| NCOA | NCO Academy: 6-week intermediate enlisted PME |
| SNCOA | Senior NCO Academy: 6.5-week senior enlisted PME |
| SOS | Squadron Officer School: company-grade officer PME |
| ACSC | Air Command and Staff College: intermediate developmental education (O-4/O-5) |
| AWC | Air War College: senior developmental education (O-5/O-6) |
| CCAF | Community College of the Air Force: associate degree program for enlisted |
| PME | Professional Military Education |
| DOR | Date of Rank: effective date of current grade |
| COE | Certificate of Eligibility: required for ANG Colonel promotions |
